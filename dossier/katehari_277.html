1Seat introductionसीट परिचय

Katehari: a close three-way seat

In 2022 SP won by 7,696 votes; in the 2024 bye-election BJP won by 34,514.

What the data says

  • 2022: 464 booths, 3,93,890 voters, turnout 62.5%.
  • 2026 draft list: 474 polling stations, 3,64,894 voters — 28,996 fewer than 2022 (7.4%).
  • Last three assembly winners: 2012 SP, 2017 BSP, 2022 SP, 2024 bye-election BJP.
  • Lok Sabha segment: 2019 BSP led by 5,254; 2024 SP led by 17,072.

Action plan

  • Every party: check its booth list against the 2026 polling-station numbers before any booth work.
  • Every party: verify each booth's voter count on the new list (claims and objections period).
  • Track the seat as a three-way contest; no party has won it twice in a row since 2012.

KPIs

  • Voters on list vs 2022
  • Booths re-mapped to 2026 numbers
  • Turnout vs 62.5% (2022)

2Ground pictureज़मीनी तस्वीर

Turnout is steady; women vote as much as men

Turnout has stayed between 60.6% and 63.2%; women were 51.5% of 2022 voters.

What the data says

  • 2022 booth turnout ranged from 15.7% to 81.2% (median 63.1%).
  • 11 booths had turnout below 50%; 47 booths had 70% or more.
  • In 332 of 464 booths more women than men voted in 2022.
  • Women's share of voters: 2012 49.1%, 2017 51.2%, 2022 51.5%.

Action plan

  • Every party: list the low-turnout booths and check if names are missing or shifted on the new roll.
  • Every party: plan women's outreach meetings in the women-majority booths (legal meetings only).
  • Keep turnout work to registration help and polling-day information — no transport or gifts to voters.

KPIs

  • Booths below 50% turnout
  • Women's share of voters
  • New-voter forms per booth

मतदान स्थिर है; महिलाएँ पुरुषों जितना वोट डालती हैं

मतदान 60.6% से 63.2% के बीच रहा; 2022 में वोट डालने वालों में 51.5% महिलाएँ थीं।

डेटा क्या कहता है

  • 2022 में बूथ-वार मतदान 15.7% से 81.2% तक (बीच का आँकड़ा 63.1%)।
  • 11 बूथों पर मतदान 50% से कम; 47 बूथों पर 70% या ज़्यादा।
  • 2022 में 464 में से 332 बूथों पर पुरुषों से ज़्यादा महिलाओं ने वोट डाला।
  • वोटरों में महिलाओं का हिस्सा: 2012 49.1%, 2017 51.2%, 2022 51.5%।

एक्शन प्लान

  • हर पार्टी: कम मतदान वाले बूथ छाँटे और देखे कि नई सूची में नाम कटे या खिसके तो नहीं।

  • मतदान बढ़ाने का काम सिर्फ़ नाम जुड़वाने और जानकारी देने तक — वोटर को वाहन या उपहार नहीं।

3Election recordचुनावी रिकॉर्ड

Winner changed in every assembly election

SP won 2012, BSP won 2017, SP won 2022, BJP won the 2024 bye-election; the margin was about 3% in both 2017 and 2022.

What the data says

  • 2012: SP 46.8%, BSP 38.1%; BJP 4.4%.
  • 2017: BSP 36.3%, BJP 33.7%, SP 19.6%, NISHAD 8.1%.
  • 2022: SP 37.9%, NISHAD (NDA) 35.0%, BSP 23.8%.
  • Lok Sabha segment: 2019 BSP 48.5% vs BJP 46.2%; 2024 SP 43.4%, BJP 36.6%, BSP 17.7%.

Action plan

  • Every party: compare its 2022 and 2024 booth results side by side (booth explorer below).
  • Every party: study the booths it lost between two elections, not only the seat total.
  • Plan for a winning line of about 38–44% of votes, the range winners needed here since 2017.

KPIs

  • Vote share vs 38% line
  • Booths won vs last election
  • Margin trend

हर विधानसभा चुनाव में विजेता बदला

2012 में सपा, 2017 में बसपा, 2022 में सपा, 2024 उपचुनाव में BJP; 2017 और 2022 दोनों में जीत का अंतर करीब 3% था।

डेटा क्या कहता है

  • 2012: सपा 46.8%, बसपा 38.1%; भाजपा 4.4%।
  • 2017: बसपा 36.3%, भाजपा 33.7%, सपा 19.6%, निषाद 8.1%।

एक्शन प्लान

  • हर पार्टी: अपने 2022 और 2024 के बूथ नतीजे साथ रखकर देखे (नीचे बूथ एक्सप्लोरर)।
  • हर पार्टी: सिर्फ़ कुल नतीजा नहीं, दो चुनावों के बीच हारे गए बूथ पढ़े।
  • जीत की रेखा ~38–44% वोट मानकर योजना बनाएँ — 2017 से यहाँ विजेता को इतना ही चाहिए था।

4Party-wise booth mapपार्टी-वार बूथ नक्शा

Booths won, second and third by party

In 2022 SP came first in 219 booths, NISHAD in 196, BSP in 51 (of 464).

What the data says

  • 2022 strong booths (60%+ share): SP 47, NISHAD 31, BSP 2.
  • 2022 weak booths (below 15%): SP 36, NISHAD 53, BSP 107.
  • 2024 Lok Sabha: SP first in 225, BJP 184, BSP 17 of 425 booths.
  • BSP was second in 178 booths in 2022 and in 80 in 2024.

Action plan

  • Every party: mark its first, second and third-place booths on one map.
  • Second-place booths are where the next gain is most likely; give them booth-level attention.
  • Weak booths (below 15%) need a basic presence first: a booth agent and a local contact.

KPIs

  • Booths in 1st place
  • Booths in 2nd place
  • Booths with an agent

Six margin bands for every party

Booths each party leads or trails by, from fortress (200+) to red alert (behind 200+).

What the data says

  • 2022 swing-lead booths (ahead by 1–99): SP 113, NISHAD 103, BSP 42.
  • 2022 can-be-flipped booths (behind by 1–99): SP 110, NISHAD 116, BSP 144.
  • Votes needed to flip all those booths in 2022: SP 5,438, NISHAD 5,703, BSP 8,684.
  • Fortress booths (200+ lead) 2022: SP 46, NISHAD 23, BSP 2; 2024: SP 56, BJP 20.

Action plan

  • Every party: defend 'swing lead' booths first — they are the easiest to lose.
  • Every party: work the 'can be flipped' booths booth by booth; the gap is under 100 votes each.
  • Do not spend effort in fortress or red-alert booths until the middle bands are covered.

KPIs

  • Swing-lead booths held
  • Can-be-flipped booths won
  • Votes gained in flip booths
